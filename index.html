2 · Braking-distance calculator

Current form and result
Start → target speed—
Deceleration / mass—
Stopping distance / time—
Kinetic energy / recovered estimate—

Convert speed to m/s, then d = (v₀² − v₁²) / (2a), t = (v₀ − v₁) / a, and ΔE = ½m(v₀² − v₁²). The recovered estimate multiplies ΔE by the selected capture-efficiency percentage. It assumes constant deceleration and is not a road-safety calculation.
