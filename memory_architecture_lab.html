Why decode? The 2-to-4 decoder turns two address bits into one active word line. A word contains four one-bit cells; the read/write signal decides whether the selected cells send their latched values out or accept new input.

Fewer, smaller gates. Two 5-to-32 decoders select one of 1,024 intersections—rather than a single 10-to-1,024 decoder. Each crossing identifies one complete word.
